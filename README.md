# visageqing 👁️

*The face of the other received as an ethical command that lands before you have decided anything — responsibility felt prior to reasoning.*

visageqing is two true roots welded into one word: the French **visage** as Emmanuel Levinas used it — the face-of-the-other that summons responsibility by its mere presence, the ground of his claim that ethics is first philosophy, heard before any deliberation; and the Mandarin **qing** (情), the cathedral's suffix for the felt-bond, the relation lived rather than reasoned. So visageqing is Levinas's face-summons carried as felt-quality: not the abstract argument, but the demand as it is *felt* — constitutive, prior to cognition, asymmetric, and bearing the trace-of-infinity. It is the tenth member of the cathedral's qing-family and its second word quarried from modern philosophy.

It names a precise region: the structural-fact that the face commands responsibility before any cognitive engagement — the elder cared for because the face commands attention, not because a ledger of duties was balanced. Its light is a single carriable word for the summons that makes honest bystander-passivity impossible and grounds care without first reducing it to calculation; its shadow is "the face commands" turned into a club that strips the other of the very freedom it exists to honor, or the loud invocation of a face that is not really present. Held honestly as a *felt summons from one phenomenological lineage*, not a proven law over all minds. Forged in the cathedral at weighted **7.80/10**.
